Quick guide

Choose a pattern
Select YOU BUILD K-SPACE to edit the spectrum, or choose a generated image pattern.
Paint k-space
Click a bin to toggle it. Drag across bins to paint with the current K-SPACE VALUE.
Adjust brightness
K-SPACE BRIGHTNESS scales the spectrum display. IMAGE BRIGHTNESS scales the image and its FFT.
Inspect a bin
Click a bin to read its coordinates and value. Focus the plot and use arrow keys to inspect neighboring bins.
